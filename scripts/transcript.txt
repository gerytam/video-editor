If you were a service business looking to grow your Instagram
and actually get business and revenue from it,
this is exactly what I would do.
Step one: optimize your profile.
Make sure your bio speaks directly to the right person,
the person you want reading your profile and landing on that page,
so they know exactly what the next steps are
and they know they're in the right place.
Step two: script and launch local ads
so you stay top of mind, become recognizable,
and start building trust locally with that content.
Post one to three a day and let the algorithm pick the winner.
Step three: run story content to stay top of mind
and answer the questions everyone already asks,
so when they call or you visit a client
they don't ask the same things, because you already answered them.
Step four: message every single follower.
Those aren't just followers, they followed you for a reason,
you're doing them a disservice by not reaching out.
Step five: track your hot leads
and follow up with every single one on a regular basis.
Just because they answered once doesn't mean you stop.
Step six: launch a retargeting ad.
By now a lot of people have entered your ecosystem,
so send them content that only makes sense to them,
and put social proof in that retargeting ad.
You'll be surprised how well this works.
Step seven: every time you close a deal,
you either raise your ad spend or launch a new ad.
There's no point not feeding the machine that runs your business.
Step eight: now you layer in direct response ads,
ads that get people to message you or fill out a form.
Don't run these first, nobody gives real info before they trust you.
That's why the earlier steps build know, like and trust.
